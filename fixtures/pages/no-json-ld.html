My Nan's Shepherd's Pie

Every Sunday my nan made this. It serves 6.

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 50 g butter

Method

  1. Brown the mince with the onion and carrots.
  2. Mash the potatoes with the butter and spread on top.
  3. Bake for 30 minutes at 200°C.
